# joshua.win/skill

A personal site of essays and write-ups, where each piece carries a hand-drawn cast and its own visual treatment.

## Content

**Piece**:
One published unit of writing on the site: an essay or a write-up.
_Avoid_: Post, article, entry

**Essay**:
A piece arguing about media or the software zeitgeist, usually around one cited work.
_Avoid_: Blog post

**Write-up**:
A technical piece about something Josh built, such as the creative-iteration skill.
_Avoid_: Case study, tutorial

**Lorem piece**:
A throwaway piece with placeholder text that tests the stack and styling, never the prose. All lorem pieces are removed before publishing.
_Avoid_: Prototype piece, dummy post

**Cited work**:
The book, film or other work a piece discusses, and the source of its cast.
_Avoid_: Source, subject

## The parade

**Cast**:
The up-to-four nouns from a piece's cited work (characters, objects, a landmark) that appear in its parade and its cast list.
_Avoid_: Characters, sprites, mascots

**Cast member**:
One noun in a cast, drawn by hand as a body on the shared legs.
_Avoid_: Character (when meaning the drawing), avatar

**Parade**:
The cast gathering along the reading-progress line as the reader passes each first mention, ending in the curtain call.
_Avoid_: Progress bar, loading bar

**Curtain call**:
The end of the parade: the cast gathers, bows from the hips, and walks off.
_Avoid_: Finale, outro

**Cast list**:
The still, linked "cast, in order of appearance" cards after a piece; the accessible version of the parade.
_Avoid_: Credits, character index

**Shared legs**:
The code-built thigh, shin and foot every cast member walks and bows on; nobody draws legs.
_Avoid_: Rig (for the legs alone)

**Rig**:
The machinery that turns a drawn body plus the shared legs into a walking, bowing cast member.
_Avoid_: Skeleton, armature

## Look

**Treatment**:
A piece's own visual design: a theme, a component map, one-offs and a layout wrapper, scoped to inside the article.
_Avoid_: Skin, template, layout

**Template**:
The complete, semantic essay or write-up page with no treatment applied.
_Avoid_: Layout, theme

**Chart playground**:
The lorem write-up's set of cheap chart prototypes for showing the creative-iteration skill's steps and orchestration.
_Avoid_: Diagram demo, chart lab

**Run file**:
The one file that holds a run's stages, agents, cards and counts, which every chart reads its content from.
_Avoid_: Chart data, fixture

**Style guide**:
The house standard (font, type scale, spacing, measure, breakpoints, motion, elevation, focus) that a treatment may override with a reason.
_Avoid_: Design system, brand

## Launch

**Deploy**:
Putting a build on the live domain, which happens from the start, behind noindex.
_Avoid_: Publish, launch (for this step)

**Publish**:
Lifting noindex (and any pre-launch privacy) once the launch bar is met.
_Avoid_: Deploy, go live

**Bake-off**:
A timeboxed head-to-head that ends in a gated choice: bake-off 1 picks the framework, bake-off 2 picks the style.
_Avoid_: Spike, evaluation

**Launch bar**:
The list of conditions, each with a definition of done, that must all hold before publishing.
_Avoid_: Deadline, release criteria

**Cut list**:
The ordered features dropped when Josh's "too much work, too little substance" signal says it's time to ship.
_Avoid_: Backlog, descope list
